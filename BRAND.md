# Saturn Energy — Brand Guidelines

*North Texas solar & climate specialists. Modern, cosmic, precise.*

---

## 1. Essence

**Saturn Energy** takes its name from the ringed planet — the most precise, elegant object in the solar system. The brand borrows that world: **orbital precision, deep-space calm, premium engineering**, brought down to a residential roof in North Texas.

The through-line for everything we make: *your home's energy, in perfect orbit.*

**Personality:** confident, exact, unhurried, quietly futuristic. Not gimmicky sci-fi — think a well-run mission control, not a cartoon rocket.

**Positioning line:** *Solar off. Roof on. Solar back.*

---

## 2. Services (three)

1. **Solar Detach & Reset (DnR)** — carefully remove a solar array for roofing work, protect it, then reinstall, reseal, rewire, and recommission it to spec.
2. **Solar Cleaning** — professional panel cleaning that clears dust, pollen, and Texas grime to restore lost production.
3. **HVAC** — high-efficiency heating & cooling installs, fast repairs, and seasonal tune-ups built for Texas extremes.

**Service area:** North Texas — Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, McKinney, Frisco, Denton and surrounding.

---

## 3. Logo

**The mark:** a minimalist Saturn — a champagne sphere crossed by a single tilted ring (~-20°), monoline, the ring passing behind the sphere at top and in front at the bottom. Restraint is the point: one planet, one ring, no clutter.

**Lockups:**
- *Primary:* mark + **SATURN** wordmark (Sora, bold, +tracking), with **ENERGY** set smaller in IBM Plex Mono beneath, letter-spaced.
- *Compact / favicon:* the ringed-planet mark alone.
- *Horizontal:* mark left, SATURN ENERGY stacked right.

**Clear space:** keep at least the ring's half-width of empty space on all sides. **Never** recolor the mark outside the palette, add a drop shadow beyond the approved soft glow, or un-tilt the ring.

---

## 4. Color

Dark-first / cosmic. The gold is Saturn's *real* pale-butterscotch hue — warmer and more muted than a typical "solar yellow."

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Space** | `#0B0F1C` | Primary background |
| **Nebula** | `#141A2C` | Lifted surfaces, cards |
| **Saturn Gold** | `#E7BD80` | Primary accent — ring, CTAs, emphasis |
| **Ring Highlight** | `#F6DCAE` | Highlights, gradients, hover |
| **Starlight** | `#ECEAE3` | Primary text on dark (warm off-white) |
| **Moon Grey** | `#949DB4` | Secondary text, captions |
| **Ion** | `#6E7BB0` | Dusty periwinkle — *graphics/starfield only*, never UI accent |
| **Hairline** | `rgba(231,189,128,0.14)` | Gold dividers, orbital rings |

Rules: spend boldness only on the **gold + the planet**; everything else stays quiet. No pure black (#000) grounds, no pure white text.

---

## 5. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / wordmark** | **Sora** (700/800) | Headlines, big statements. Geometric, modern. |
| **Body** | **Hanken Grotesk** (400/500) | Running text. Clean, warm, ~65-char measure. |
| **Technical labels** | **IBM Plex Mono** (500) | Eyebrows, "orbital readouts," stats, coordinates — UPPERCASE, wide tracking (`STATION 01 · DETACH`, `N 33.0° · NORTH TEXAS`). |

All three are Google Fonts. Headings get `text-wrap: balance`.

---

## 6. Motifs & imagery

- **Orbital rings** — thin concentric gold hairlines, elliptical arcs, ring dividers between sections.
- **Starfield** — faint, sparse, low-opacity; subtle twinkle at most. Never busy.
- **Mission-control labels** — mono coordinates and station numbers give a sense of precision.
- **Imagery** — hyperreal, cinematic, golden-hour. Real crews in Saturn gear on real North Texas roofs. Compose action to one side so type has room to breathe.
- **Motion** — deliberate and restrained: a slow-rotating hero ring, scroll-reveal fade-ups, gentle hover states. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 7. Voice

Active, specific, confident — written from the homeowner's side. Say exactly what happens: *"We remove every panel, label it, and reset it good as new."* No hype, no apologies, no jargon. Cosmic metaphors are seasoning, not the meal.
